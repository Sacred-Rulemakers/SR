SR DIRECTORY / REQUESTS

Requests

Information about proposed additions, amendments, corrections and interpretations concerning the Standard Rulebook.

Current status

Formal submissions are not open.

The site does not currently provide a request form, submission tracking or a formal decision process. A post in the Forums is a public discussion, not an official request or a promise of review.

Matters that may be discussed

Proposed addition

A suggestion for a new Rule or a new provision within an existing Book, Part or Section.

Amendment

A suggested change to the wording, scope or placement of an existing Rule.

Correction

A report of a numbering, spelling, formatting or cross-reference issue in the published text.

Interpretation

A question about how a particular Rule is worded or how it relates to another provision.

Where to discuss an idea

The Forums include a Proposals category for public discussion of suggested changes. Members can open a discussion there and identify the relevant Rule number or section. Readers without an account can read public discussions; an account is required to create a post or reply.

Keep submissions focused on the text. Include the Rule number where possible, explain the issue, and distinguish the current wording from the change being suggested. Do not include private or sensitive information.

Visit the Forums →

No formal decision is implied

Discussion does not automatically amend the Rulebook. The published text remains the current edition unless a revised publication is explicitly issued. The site currently has no public request register, status tracker, acceptance criteria or timetable for decisions.
