Access and Data

How to use the site, what the forum stores and where the site's services are provided.

Access and accessibility

The public pages and Rulebook reader are available without a forum account. An account is needed to start a discussion, reply, react or submit a report. The forum sign-in form uses an email address and password.

The site uses responsive layouts for smaller screens, semantic page headings and navigation landmarks, labels for form fields, and visible keyboard-focus styles. The Rulebook reader can be searched and browsed with its contents navigation. These features are intended to make the site easier to use, but the website has not been independently certified as conforming to a particular accessibility standard.

Some interactive forum features require JavaScript. The Supabase client library is loaded from a third-party content delivery service; if that service is unavailable, forum sign-in and database features may not work.

Information used by the site

The static pages are hosted through GitHub Pages. The Forums use Supabase for account authentication and forum data. The forum interface uses the following information:

Account and sign-in
An email address and authentication data are handled by Supabase when an account is registered or used to sign in. The forum interface does not display members' email addresses publicly.
Profile
A display name is required and appears alongside forum contributions. The account role and join date are available to signed-in forum members. A short biography and avatar image URL are optional. Biography, avatar URL and last-active time are stored separately from the public-facing profile record: members can access their own details, while authorised forum administrators can view them through the member directory.
Activity
The forum records a last-active timestamp when a signed-in member has the forum open and the page is active. Administrators can use this to see whether an account has been active recently.
Discussions and replies
Discussion titles and messages, replies, authorship and timestamps are stored so that the public forum can display them. Public discussions and replies should be treated as public information.
Reactions and reports
Reaction choices and report details are stored to operate the forum and moderation process. Reports are available to the moderation team.
Saved Rules
The Rulebook's Save feature stores selected Rule numbers in local browser storage on the device being used. These saved items are not synchronised to a forum account.

Visibility and third-party services

Forum discussions and replies are intended for public reading. Do not include passwords, personal contact details, private correspondence or information about another person in public posts or reports unless there is a clear and appropriate reason to do so.

GitHub Pages provides website hosting. Supabase provides forum authentication and data services. The Supabase JavaScript library is delivered through jsDelivr. Avatar images use the image address supplied by the member and may be served by an external provider. Those services operate under their own terms and privacy information.

The site does not publish member email addresses in its public forum interface. This should not be taken as a guarantee that information shared with a third-party service is known only to the SR.

Your choices

You can choose not to create a forum account and still read the public pages and discussions. You can leave the optional biography and avatar fields empty. You can remove saved Rule bookmarks through the Rulebook reader or by clearing the relevant site data in your browser.

There is no automated account-deletion or data-export control currently provided on the site. Do not submit information that you would not be comfortable having stored by the forum service. The site does not state a fixed retention period for forum records.

Questions or access difficulties

Formal support and privacy-request forms are not currently available. For a general site issue, use the Forums without including private account information. Do not post passwords, authentication codes or other credentials.
