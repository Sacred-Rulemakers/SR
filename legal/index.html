The institutional names, offices, ranks and procedures used on this website are part of that presentation. They do not represent a government department, court, regulator, statutory authority or public institution.

The Standard Rulebook is a creative publication. Its Rules are not legislation, professional guidance, legal advice or instructions that create real-world legal obligations.

Using this website

Visitors may read the public pages and Rulebook. Forum members may use the account features to take part in discussions. When using the site, do not attempt to disrupt its operation, access another person's account, impersonate another person, harass other members, spam the forum or submit material that you do not have permission to share.

Do not use public discussions to publish passwords, authentication codes, private contact details or confidential information. Public posts may be read, copied or retained by other visitors.

Forum contributions and moderation

Members are responsible for the material they submit. Only share text, images and other material that you are entitled to share. Do not assume that a forum post is private or that deleting or editing a post removes copies already made by other people.

Moderators and administrators may review reports and use the controls provided by the forum to remove content, lock or pin discussions, and manage account roles within their permissions. These controls exist to maintain the forum and do not confer authority outside the website.

Publication and attribution

The Standard Rulebook and other site material are presented as publications of the SR project. The site does not grant a general permission to reproduce, republish or commercially use material from it. Seek permission before reproducing substantial portions of the publication or presenting them as your own work.

Short quotations for commentary or discussion should be clearly attributed to the relevant Rule and publication where practical.

Third-party services and availability

The website is hosted through GitHub Pages. Forum authentication and forum data are provided through Supabase, and the browser loads the Supabase client library from jsDelivr. These services are operated independently and are subject to their own terms and availability.

The site may be changed, interrupted or withdrawn. No guarantee is made that every feature will be continuously available or that information will remain unchanged.

For information about data used by the site, see Access and Data.
