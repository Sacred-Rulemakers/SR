SR DIRECTORY / MANAGEMENT

Management

The account roles and moderation permissions used to operate the SR public forums.

Scope of management

This page describes permissions inside the forum software only. A forum role does not appoint someone to a real-world office, give them authority over the wider project or create powers outside this website.

Roles are assigned to individual forum accounts. The controls available to an account depend on its current role, and the database checks role changes rather than relying only on what the interface displays.

Account roles

Owner

The highest forum-management role. An owner can review reports, use the moderation controls and change another account's role among owner, administrator, moderator and member. An owner cannot change their own role through the member directory.

Administrator

An administrator can review reports and use the moderation controls. Administrators can promote members to moderator and can demote moderators to member. They cannot appoint or change an owner or administrator.

Moderator

A moderator can review reports and use the available discussion and reply moderation controls, including removing content and locking or pinning discussions. Moderators cannot manage member roles.

Member

A member can participate in the public forum: start discussions, reply, react, report content and edit their own contributions where the relevant controls are available.

Moderation principles

Reports are reviewed by the moderation team. A report is a request for review, not a finding that a rule has been broken. Moderators may remove material from public view or restrict a discussion when needed to keep the forum usable.

Role changes should be made deliberately. The interface asks for confirmation before applying a change, and the database rejects changes that exceed the acting account's permissions or attempt to change that account's own role.

Member directory

Administrators and the owner can open the member directory from the Forums' Moderation panel. It provides profile information and contribution statistics, including discussions, replies and reactions. Recent activity is calculated from the last-active timestamp recorded by the forum.

The member directory is a management tool, not a public ranking. Activity counts are descriptive and should not be treated as a measure of a person's value or standing.
