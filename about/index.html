About the SR

The project, its publication and the public spaces maintained around the Standard Rulebook.

Purpose

The Sacred Rulemakers (SR) is an independent creative project built around the Standard Rulebook: a formal, numbered collection of rules presented as the work of an institution. Its language, offices and procedures form part of the project's presentation.

The project is intended for reading, writing, discussion and amusement. The institutional style is not a claim to governmental, statutory, regulatory or other public authority, and the Rulebook does not create real-world legal duties.

The Standard Rulebook

The Rulebook is the central publication of the SR. It is arranged into 15 Books, 34 Parts and 77 Sections, containing 770 consecutively numbered Rules. The online reader supports browsing by Book, searching by wording or number, copying individual Rules and saving selected Rules in the current browser.

The numbering provides stable references for discussion. A saved Rule is a local browser bookmark; it is not a formal endorsement or amendment.

Project status

The Rulebook reader and public forum are available through this site. Forum registration, discussions, replies, reactions and moderation tools are connected to the forum service. A formal request-submission workflow is not yet available; the Requests page explains the current alternative for public discussion.

Pages describing the site's operation are updated as features change. The presence of a page in the directory does not imply that every proposed service is active.
